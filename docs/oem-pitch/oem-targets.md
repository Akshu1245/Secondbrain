# OEM targets — ranked, with entry points

This is the order to pitch in. Logic for each ranking is the *combination* of
(a) loosest grip on stock Android, (b) weakest current memory layer, (c) most
public evidence the OEM is asking for exactly what AOL provides, and (d)
realistic chance of getting a 15-minute meeting in 2025–2026.

> **Do not pitch Samsung or Apple.** Galaxy AI / Apple Intelligence run on
> locked stacks and 2-3 year roadmaps. The cost of getting in is more than
> the value of getting in.

## Ranked targets

### 1. Motorola (Lenovo) — **the #1 target**

**Why:** Moto AI was rebooted in 2024 with a feature umbrella (Catch Me Up,
Pay Attention, Remember This, LAM proof-of-concept, Perplexity integration,
Image AI). The branding is fresh and the tech is *demonstrably* dependent on
3rd-party AI (Gemini + Perplexity), which means a partnership precedent
already exists. User complaints converge on three things AOL fixes directly:
bloatware perception, swipe-up gesture failure, and a missing memory layer
between "Remember This" sessions.

**Entry points:**

- Lenovo Innovation World (annual event, has historically picked up
  student/independent projects).
- Motorola India dev-relations on LinkedIn (search: *"Motorola India"
  developer relations* OR *Lenovo India AI*).
- Cold email to Moto AI product managers, leading with the live demo
  + buildable APK (search: *"Product Manager Motorola AI"*).

**Why-they-listen line:** *"Moto AI ships memory features that don't persist.
We built the persistence layer + an optimisation layer. 15 minutes."*

### 2. OnePlus — **stage 2 of their public roadmap is literally what we built**

**Why:** OnePlus's public 3-stage AI strategy names stage 2 *"Your Secondary
Mind"* — a Mind Space + LLM persona that understands all your content. AOL
+ Second Brain *is* that. Carl Pei alumnus culture, Bengaluru office, deep
hardware-software integration mindset. Opportunity: pitch *exactly* their
roadmap with a working demo and shave 12 months off their build time.

**Entry points:**

- OnePlus India (Bengaluru) — software / product team.
- OPPO–OnePlus AI alliance (cross-org).
- Public Carl Pei alumni network (Nothing has bled engineers from OnePlus,
  many remain reachable).

**Why-they-listen line:** *"You announced 'Your Secondary Mind'. Here's a
working demo of what stage 2 looks like. Want to compare notes?"*

### 3. Nothing — **founder is unusually open to 3rd-party AI**

**Why:** Carl Pei has publicly talked about the *end of the app era* and the
move to contextual AI. Nothing OS is small, fast-moving, and has shipped
3rd-party integrations before. AOL is exactly the contextual middleware Carl
keeps describing.

**Entry points:**

- Carl Pei on X / LinkedIn (he replies to high-signal DMs).
- Nothing's developer-relations contact in London / India.
- Nothing Community (the official forum has Carl + senior team active).

**Why-they-listen line:** *"You said apps are dead. Here's the system layer
that replaces the app drawer for AI features. 90-second demo."*

### 4. ASUS / ROG — **creator-audience leverage**

**Why:** ROG Phone's audience is creators, gamers, and power users — exactly
the demographic that *will* tweak their AI surface, prioritise compute, and
care about second-brain workflows. ASUS already builds tightly around its
Armoury Crate / dial-and-knob power-user UX, so a Control Panel like AOL fits
their existing brand language.

**Entry points:**

- ASUS Open Platform / partner developer programme.
- ROG India team (Bengaluru / Taipei).

### 5. Jio / Reliance (JioPhone Next + AI Phone) — **India-first, language-first**

**Why:** Reliance is building an India-first AI phone with Indian-language
emphasis. AOL's rule-based filter + on-device routing dovetails with the
"data-saver / battery-saver / private-mode" preferences that matter
disproportionately on entry-level hardware in India. AOL is operator-friendly
(it reduces compute spend at scale).

**Entry points:**

- Jio Platforms Limited startup connect / partnerships.
- Reliance Foundation incubator.

## Companies to *not* pitch (and why)

| Company | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Samsung** | Galaxy AI is locked behind Samsung Knox + Samsung's own model partnerships. The partner pipeline is 18-30 months. Don't waste cycles. |
| **Apple** | Apple Intelligence runs on Private Cloud Compute + custom silicon. Zero appetite for 3rd-party middleware on the AI path. |
| **Google (Pixel)** | Gemini *is* the AI layer. Google would rebuild this in-house if the idea travelled. Wrong audience. |
| **Vivo / iQOO** | Possible later, but their AI strategy is opaque outside China and no public OEM-AI complaint surface exists in EN markets yet. |

## Pitch order summary

1. Motorola → 2. OnePlus → 3. Nothing → 4. ASUS / ROG → 5. Jio.

If 1–3 don't reply within two weeks of personalised outreach (templates in
[`oem-outreach.md`](./oem-outreach.md)), proceed to 4 and 5 in parallel. If
*all five* go quiet, the fallback is hackathon submission (Lenovo Innovation
World, MWC Barcelona Innovation Award, Snapdragon On-Device AI Challenge),
which ladders back to Motorola anyway.
